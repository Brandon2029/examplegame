Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can return south to the living room.
#
Office
Your father's office smells faintly of paper and old books. 
A computer sits on a desk beside stacks of folders. 
The blinds are half closed, leaving the room dim even during the day.
The living room is east. A passage south leads toward the garage.
#
Kitchen
The kitchen has a crowded counter, a humming refrigerator, and a table covered with yesterday's mail. 
A flashlight, a knife, and a mysterious orb are here, waiting to be examined.
You can head west to the living room or south to the garage.
#
Attic
The attic is cramped and quiet. Boxes line the walls beneath sloping rafters, and a small safe is hidden behind a pile of old blankets. 
Dust floats through a narrow beam of sunlight.The only way out is down to the bedroom.
#
Garage
The garage contains your father's car, shelves of supplies, and scattered tools. 
The air smells of rubber and motor oil. You have reached the place that holds the goal of your adventure.
You can go north to the kitchen or up toward the office.
